Maison du Sport et de la Santé Interview

Video interview production

Context

Produced as part of a group assignment in MMI (2nd year), each group had to interview a beneficiary of the Maison du Sport et de la Santé in Elbeuf, along with an adapted physical activity instructor. The interviews were carried out in collaboration with the Maison du Sport et de la Santé, who will publish them on their social media channels.

Process

Production & Project Management

We formed a team of five, and I served as project manager (elected totally democratically, of course, haha). I assigned roles to the team members and guided them as best as I could.

Filming took place directly on-site at the Maison du Sport et de la Santé.

As for me, I handled audio recording as boom operator. To capture sound, we used a Zoom H4n recorder and an omnidirectional microphone. For video, we set up two Canon EOS 5D Mark III cameras.

Post-production editing was done in Adobe Premiere Pro, where we experimented with a new workflow: multi-camera editing, which brings dynamic rhythm and makes the interview much more engaging. While the project was carried out as a team, editing was completely individual, so this edit is entirely my own! I chose to cut out the segments where Arthur, the interviewer, was asking questions to keep the video as concise as possible.
